# **E-Commerce Sales \& Profit Analysis**



## **Project Overview**

### This project aims to analyze e-commerce sales data to understand sales performance, profitability, and product trends.

# 

## **Problem Statement**

### Businesses need to understand which products and categories generate the most sales and profit. Data analysis can help identify trends and support better business decisions.

# 

## **Objectives**

* ### Analyze total sales and profit.
* ### Identify top-performing products and categories.
* ### Explore monthly sales trends.
* ### Compare performance across regions.
* ### Create an interactive Power BI dashboard.

# &#x20;

## **Tools and Technologies Used**



* ### Python – Data analysis and visualization
* ### Pandas – Data cleaning and manipulation
* ### Matplotlib – Creating charts and graphs
* ### Power BI – Interactive dashboard development
* ### Microsoft Excel – Data inspection and preparation
* ### Git and GitHub – Version control and project documentation



## 

## **Project Workflow**



### 1\. Dataset selection and inspection.

### 2\. Data cleaning and preparation using Python.

### 3\. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).

### 4\. Data visualization using Python.

### 5\. Interactive dashboard creation in Power BI.

### 6\. Business insights and recommendations.

### 7\. Project documentation and version control using GitHub.

## **Power BI Dashboard**



## The dashboard helps users explore sales and profit performance through:

## 

1. ### Total Sales, Total Profit and Total Quantity cards.
2. ### Sales by Category.
3. ### Profit by Category.
4. ### Sales by Region.
5. ### Profit by Sub-Category.
6. ### Sales by Segment.
7. ### Ship Mode and State slicers for interactive filtering.







## **Key Business Insights**



### Compare sales and profit across product categories.

### Identify regions and customer segments with strong sales performance.

### Examine profit differences between product sub-categories.

### Analyze the relationship between discounts and profitability.

### Identify products or areas that may require further investigation.

### 

## 

## **Recommendations**



### 1\. Review discount policies to understand their effect on profit margins.

### 2\. Investigate low-profit or loss-making sub-categories before making pricing decisions.

### 3\. Study high-performing regions and customer segments to identify growth opportunities.

### 4\. Monitor sales and profit together rather than focusing on sales alone.

### 5\. Use interactive dashboard filters to support data-driven business decisions.







## **Conclusion**



### This project demonstrates an end-to-end data analytics workflow, from data preparation and exploratory analysis to visualization and business recommendations. Python and Power BI help transform sales data into useful information for business decision-making.
